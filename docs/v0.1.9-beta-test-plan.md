# Snapmaker U1 Card v0.1.9-beta.1 — validation checklist

> DEVELOPMENT ONLY. Do not merge into main or publish a stable release before validation.

## Scope
- Expandable camera overlay with fullscreen support and refresh.
- SVG graphs of power (W), sampled job energy (kWh), and electricity cost (€).
- Adaptive ETA based on recent progress and printer-provided time remaining.

## HACS distribution
- Keep `snapmaker-u1-card.js` as the HACS entrypoint.
- Keep `custom:snapmaker-u1-card` unchanged.
- Create a GitHub **pre-release** tagged `v0.1.9-beta.1` pointing to the tested commit when ready.
- HACS may install a beta over v0.1.8; it is **not** a parallel installation. Back up the installed version or plan to reinstall v0.1.8.
- `snapmaker-u1-card-dev.js` is for independent testing and must not replace the HACS entrypoint.

## Acceptance tests
- [ ] Existing v0.1.8 configuration works without new required YAML.
- [ ] Powered-off state and power relay transitions behave correctly.
- [ ] Camera opens, closes, refreshes, enters and exits fullscreen; Escape works.
- [ ] LED brightness follows the Home Assistant entity without forcing brightness.
- [ ] Charts collect samples during printing and do not claim historical data while the card was closed.
- [ ] kWh and electricity costs remain coherent after pause/resume and completion.
- [ ] Job change resets counters without leaking the prior job.
- [ ] ETA starts with native firmware estimate, refines after sufficient progress, and does not update while paused.
- [ ] Test desktop and mobile layouts.
- [ ] Test HACS upgrade and rollback to v0.1.8.

## Known limitations to evaluate
- The energy chart currently requires the card to remain open for sampling.
- Progress-based ETA is volatile during tool changes and nonuniform layers; validate against real prints.
- Camera overlay DOM can be replaced by a full card re-render.
